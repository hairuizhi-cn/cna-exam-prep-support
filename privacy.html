Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy describes how the CNA Exam Prep Buddy iOS app handles information. The app does not require an account and does not operate its own analytics, advertising, or progress-sync backend.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following study data locally on your device:

  • Answers, accuracy, missed questions, bookmarks, and per-question attempt counts.
  • Practice and exam drafts, exam history, study days, streaks, goals, badges, and XP.
  • Settings such as your daily goal, exam date, instant-feedback preference, appearance, reminder time, and free-feature usage.

This information is used to provide the app's practice, progress, resume, and reminder features. It is not transmitted to a server operated by CNA Exam Prep Buddy.

Purchases and Pro access

Optional Pro access is sold through Apple StoreKit as non-renewing subscriptions for one month, three months, or one year. These purchases do not auto-renew.

Apple processes your payment and Apple ID transaction information. The app uses verified StoreKit transactions to determine and restore access and to calculate its expiration date. We do not receive your full payment card number. Purchase records are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

Study reminders

If you enable study reminders, the app schedules local notifications at the time you choose. Reminder permissions and delivery are handled by iOS. These are not push notifications from our server, and enabling them does not send your study schedule to us.

Information you choose to send

If you email 1095288658@qq.com for support or privacy questions, we receive your email address and the content you send. This information is used to respond to your request. Your email provider processes that correspondence under its own policies.

Do not send your Apple ID password, full payment card details, or other sensitive credentials.

Data we do not collect by default

  • No account registration or profile.
  • No third-party advertising or advertising identifier.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting backend operated by us.
  • No sale of personal information.
  • No collection of precise location, contacts, photos, health records, or clinical patient information.

Retention and deletion

Local study data remains on your device until you reset practice progress in Settings, delete the app, or erase the relevant device data. Resetting practice progress does not cancel or alter Apple purchase records. Apple retains transaction information according to its own policies.

Support emails are retained as needed to handle your request and maintain a support record. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable record-keeping requirement.

Children

The app is intended for adults preparing for a nurse aide exam and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If future versions add features such as cloud sync, analytics, or crash reporting, this policy and the app's App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before those features are released. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information can be sent to 1095288658@qq.com.
