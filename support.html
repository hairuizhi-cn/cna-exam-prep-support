Purchases and Pro access

How do I restore a previous purchase?

Open the app's Settings tab and tap Restore Purchases in the Pro access section. Use the same Apple ID that made the purchase and keep the device connected to the internet. The paywall also includes a Restore Purchases button.

Do Pro plans renew automatically?

No. Pro is offered as a non-renewing subscription for one month, three months, or one year. You pay once for the selected access period. It does not auto-renew, and you can purchase another period when access ends.

Who handles payment and refunds?

Purchases are processed by Apple through your Apple ID. The app does not collect your full payment card number. Refund eligibility and requests are handled through Apple.

Practice and exam sessions

Can I continue an unfinished session?

Practice and exam drafts are saved independently when you leave a session or the app enters the background. Use the continue card on the Practice or Exam tab. Discarding a draft cannot be undone.

Does the exam timer stop in the background?

No. Timed exams use a fixed wall-clock deadline. Time continues to elapse while the app is in the background, and the exam finishes when the deadline is reached.

How are missed questions determined?

A question enters the missed list after an incorrect or unanswered exam result and is removed after you answer it correctly on a later attempt. The list reflects the latest attempt, not your entire answer history.

Progress and data

Where is my study progress stored?

Progress is stored locally on your device. The current version does not provide an account or cloud progress-sync service. Deleting the app or erasing device data may remove your progress.

How do I reset practice progress?

Go to Settings → Data → Reset practice progress. This clears study progress, missed items, bookmarks, streaks, and exam history. Your subscription status and free-exam usage are not reset.

Study reminders

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Enable Settings → Notifications → Study reminders in the app and allow notifications when iOS asks. Also check notification permission for CNA Exam Prep in the iOS Settings app. Reminders are local notifications and do not require an account.
